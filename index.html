BUILD STUFF. BREAK IT SAFELY.

PEN-TEST is a Hack Club cybersecurity YSWS built for curious teens who want to learn by doing, not by watching. We combine community, projects, and hands-on security practice.

Instead of only reading about cyber, you’ll ship projects, document your process, and learn how security works in the real world with other builders from your club.

FAQ

Everything you need to know before jumping into PEN-TEST.

What tools can I use?

Wireshark, Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Hashcat, Volatility, Autopsy, BeEF — anything you like as long as it is for learning, research, or authorized testing.

How many submissions can I make?

As many as you want!

Can I use AI?

AI is not allowed. We want to see your work, not AI-generated shortcuts.

Can I participate?

Any teenager ages 13–18 (under 19) can join! We are especially welcoming to beginners.

Can I join even if I can't code?

Absolutely. Beginners are more than welcome in PEN-TEST — curiosity matters as much as experience.

Can I submit my project to another YSWS?

Double-dipping isn't allowed, sorry!

How do I get into the dashboard?

Use the Enter button to access the dashboard and shop.

Can I submit team projects?

No, sorry! Personal projects only.
