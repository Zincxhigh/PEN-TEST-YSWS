LEARN BY BUILDING.

PEN-TEST is a Hack Club cybersecurity YSWS built around practical learning and experimentation.

Instead of simply reading about cybersecurity, you'll create projects, document your progress, and learn how security works in the real world.

WHAT WILL YOU BUILD?

01

WEB SECURITY

Explore vulnerabilities and learn how modern web applications can be secured.

02

NETWORK SECURITY

Understand networks, traffic, protocols, and defensive security concepts.

03

CRYPTOGRAPHY

Explore the techniques used to protect information and communications.

04

OSINT

Learn how publicly available information can be researched responsibly.

05

DIGITAL FORENSICS

Discover how digital evidence can be analyzed during investigations.

06

INCIDENT RESPONSE

Learn how security teams detect, investigate, and respond to incidents.

FAQ

Everything you need to know before jumping into PEN-TEST.

What tools can I use?

Wireshark, Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Hashcat, Volatility, Autopsy, BeEF anything you like as long as it's for a project and not a for malicous use

How many submissions can I make?

As many as you want!

Can I use AI?

AI is not allowed. We want to see your work not AI.

Can I participate?

Any teenager ages 13–18 (under 19) can join!

Can I join even if I can't code?

Beginners are more than welcome to participate in PEN-TEST.

Can I submit my project to another YSWS?

Double-dipping isn't allowed, sorry!

How do I register for the CTF?

Hit Register with Hack Club — we use your Hack Club identity to create your CTF account and log you straight into the arena.

Can I submit team projects?

No, sorry!
